# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Guest-post operations staff using the CRM throughout the workday to manage contacts, conversations, deals, orders, reminders, and related workflows across desktop and mobile-sized screens.

## Product Purpose

GuestPostCRM centralizes operational guest-post work in a familiar, efficient dashboard. Success means users can find records, act on messages, monitor activity, configure layouts, and access supporting tools without losing their place.

## Brand Personality

Efficient, familiar, and focused. The interface should feel dependable and approachable while prioritizing fast operational work over decorative presentation.

## Anti-references

Avoid broad visual redesigns, hidden primary actions, unfamiliar navigation patterns, unnecessary decoration, and changes that disrupt established workflows or information density.

## Design Principles

- Keep high-value actions discoverable at every supported breakpoint.
- Match established component and interaction patterns before introducing new ones.
- Preserve user context when configuring or opening supporting workflows.
- Make responsive behavior structural and predictable.
- Limit changes to the smallest surface needed to solve the user problem.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve semantic controls, visible labels, keyboard interaction, focus behavior, and comfortable touch targets. Do not rely on color alone to communicate an action or state.
